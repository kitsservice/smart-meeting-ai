2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide AI-powered transcription, diarization, and meeting intelligence, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Audio Recordings & Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Data Collected: Audio streams recorded through your device microphone during active meeting sessions or voice calibration.
  • Purpose: Used exclusively to convert spoken audio into written text, perform real-time speech diarization, and extract key action items.
  • Foreground Service: A foreground notification service is utilized while recording to ensure uninterrupted audio capture when your screen is locked or while multitasking.
  • Retention: Temporary audio buffers on device storage are automatically purged after transcription is verified and saved.

B. Voice Biometrics & Speaker Signatures

  • Data Collected: A 192-dimensional numerical vector (acoustic embedding) generated from a short 2–3 second user voice sample.
  • Purpose: Used strictly within your meetings to distinguish your voice from other meeting participants and correctly attribute your name to spoken segments.
  • Security: The biometric embedding is a one-way mathematical abstraction from which original raw voice or identifiable speech cannot be reverse-engineered. It is stored securely in encrypted cloud storage and never shared with third-party advertisers.

C. Meeting Content & Transcripts

  • Data Collected: Generated transcripts, meeting titles, descriptions, executive summaries, decisions, and assigned action items.
  • Purpose: To allow you to review past meeting notes, search transcripts semantically, query the Ares AI meeting assistant, and synchronize meetings across your signed-in devices.

D. Account & Profile Information

  • Data Collected: Your email address, display name, and unique user ID provided when you register or sign in using Supabase Authentication or Google OAuth.
  • Purpose: To authenticate your account, secure your meetings with Row Level Security (RLS), and personalize speaker names.

E. Device & Diagnostics Data

  • Data Collected: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and crash stack traces collected anonymously via Sentry.
  • Purpose: Strictly for technical troubleshooting, performance optimization, and bug fixing.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Data Processors)

We work with trusted industry-leading cloud service providers to power specific technical functions of the Application. All third parties are contractually bound by confidentiality and data processing agreements:

  • Deepgram Inc.: Cloud-based automatic speech recognition (ASR) and speaker diarization engine.
  • Sarvam AI: Specialized Indic language processing for Hindi, Marathi, and code-mixed speech recognition and conversational intelligence.
  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud PostgreSQL database, user authentication, and secure encrypted storage.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): Crash reporting and application stability monitoring.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Encryption

We implement rigorous security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application, our backend APIs, and cloud services is encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 protocols.
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored database records, transcripts, and voice embeddings are encrypted at rest with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Control: Database tables are strictly protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS), ensuring that only the authenticated owner can access their meeting data.

5. User Data Rights and Complete Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data Deletion Policy and global privacy standards, you maintain absolute control over your personal data:

In-App Account & Data Deletion:
You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app at any time:
  1. Open Smart Meeting AI on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Everything.
  4. Confirm deletion.
Upon confirmation, your user profile, voice biometrics signature, all meeting transcripts, summaries, action items, and audio files are immediately and permanently deleted from our cloud databases. This action is irreversible.

You may also submit a manual deletion request by contacting us at kitsserviceakola@gmail.com. Manual requests are fulfilled within 48 business hours.
